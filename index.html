About

Balancing the books with no fuss

I’m a qualified, licensed AAT bookkeeper with experience working in a chartered accountancy practice, as well as first-hand experience of running my own small businesses. So I understand bookkeeping from both sides — the importance of getting the numbers right, but also the reality of running a busy small business where there never seem to be enough hours in the day.

I only work with local businesses in Chichester and surrounding areas, so it’s easy to meet in person if there’s anything you want to discuss face to face.
